04 — Rules

How to use

Keep the logo legible, consistent, and recognizable in every context. The mark is the most important asset of the brand — these guidelines protect it.

Use on white

Default usage. White background gives the primary mark maximum contrast and clarity.

Use on Fellow Blue

Switch to the dark lockup whenever the background is Fellow Blue or darker than #6B7785.

Use on neutral surfaces

Light neutrals under #DCE0E6 are safe. Keep clearspace, even when colors are subtle.

Don't stretch or distort

Always scale the logo proportionally. Never squash, skew, or rotate the mark.

Don't recolor the wordmark

The wordmark only renders in black or white. Do not apply gradients, hue shifts, or off-palette colors.

Don't crowd the mark

Respect the clearspace defined in section 05. Other elements must stay outside that boundary.

05 — Specs

Clearspace & sizing

Always reserve enough breathing room around the mark, and never use it smaller than the minimum legible size. The unit x equals the height of the chevron bracket.
